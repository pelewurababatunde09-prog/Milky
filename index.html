Questions

Are the free templates really free?

Yes. You can use them in your own projects without paying. The only restriction is that you can't resell or redistribute them as templates.

Can I use a template for a client?

Yes. Free templates can be used for client work. For premium templates, check the licence that comes with the template.

Are the templates accessible?

They're built with semantic HTML and keyboard-friendly patterns. Always test with your own content, since changing colours or text can affect contrast and readability.
