Director's note

I came to photography through cinema. I still think in scenes, in colour palettes, in what happens just outside the edge of the frame. My images are quiet on purpose, muted in colour and patient in rhythm.

My work has appeared in independent magazines, short-film campaigns and gallery shows. Every project begins with a conversation, because good images start with understanding the story.

“It felt like being in a film, not in front of a camera. The images are exactly how that day felt.”Client testimonial, brand campaign

How a shoot unfolds

  1. Brief

    We talk through the story, the mood and where the images will live.

  2. Scout

    I find the light and the location, and share a visual reference board.

  3. Shoot

    Calm, unhurried and mostly natural light, so you can be yourself.

  4. Grade

    Each frame is colour-graded by hand to one consistent, muted look.

  5. Deliver

    A private gallery with high-resolution files, ready to use.

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

Four to six weeks is ideal for most sessions. For editorial and campaign work, please enquire earlier.

When will I receive my images?

Portrait galleries are delivered within 10 working days. Larger projects are scheduled individually.

Can I use the images commercially?

Personal use is included with portrait sessions. Commercial usage is covered by the licence in your editorial or campaign agreement.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule free of charge up to 72 hours before your shoot. A 30% deposit secures your date.
