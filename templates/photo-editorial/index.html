Selected work

I wait for the in-between moments.

My work has appeared in independent magazines, gallery shows and brand campaigns. Every session begins with a conversation, because a good photograph starts with understanding.

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

Four to six weeks is ideal for most sessions. For editorial and commission work, please enquire earlier.

When will I receive my images?

Portrait galleries are delivered within 10 working days. Larger projects are scheduled individually.

Can I use the images commercially?

Personal use is included with portrait sessions. Commercial usage is covered by the licence in your editorial or commission agreement.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule free of charge up to 72 hours before your session. A 30% deposit secures your date.
