Everything starts with the fire.

We cook over oak and olive wood because it is the one ingredient that makes everything taste like itself, only more. The menu is short, changes with the weeks, and leans on the growers and fishermen we know by name.

Come hungry, share plates and stay longer than you meant to.

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. The bar and a few tables are kept for walk-ins every evening, though a reservation is the safest way to guarantee a seat.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes. Many dishes are vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free, and we are happy to adapt others. Please tell us when you book and again when you arrive.

Is there a dress code?

No. Come as you are.

Can I order takeaway?

Selected dishes are available to collect. Call us during opening hours to order.

How do I cancel or change a booking?

Reply to your confirmation email or call us, ideally at least 24 hours ahead.

Hear about the new menu first.

One email when the menu changes or a special evening is announced.
