Inside the atelier

Nothing leaves the studio until it moves the way it should. This is the path every piece takes.

  • Sketch

    Every piece starts as a drawing and a conversation about how you want to feel in it.

  • Pattern

    Patterns are cut by hand to your proportions, not adjusted from a standard size.

  • Toile

    A trial version in plain cotton lets us perfect the line before we touch the final cloth.

  • Fitting

    We refine the garment on you, adjusting balance, length and ease until it sits right.

  • Finish

    Hand-stitched hems, pressed seams and fastenings set by hand complete each piece.

Good to know

How do I find my size?

Use our for ready to wear. For made to measure, we take your measurements in person or guide you through taking them at home.

How long does a made to measure piece take?

Allow six to ten weeks from your first fitting. Bridal and evening commissions are scheduled individually, so please enquire early.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Delivery times and costs are confirmed when you order. Read the for details.

Can I return or alter a piece?

Ready to wear can be returned within 14 days if unworn. Made to measure pieces are made for you, so we offer alterations instead of returns.

How should I care for my garment?

Every piece arrives with a care card. As a rule, dry clean tailoring, hand wash silk and linen, and steam rather than iron where you can.

Made with care in small batches. We work with mills that publish where their cloth comes from, and we keep offcuts for future pieces.
